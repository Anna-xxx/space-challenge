Hi! Update for Sputnik 2.0 (14.11), based on the official challenge resources (NASA HRP Hazards, Risks, Ongoing research; CSA).

1. Weekly vision check (SANS, NASA's leading spaceflight health risk). Once a week on the tablet: Amsler grid + simple acuity test (~1 min) and the question "Has your vision changed?". Deviation from the first check or "yes" sends a note to crew medic Maria. No diagnosis. Threshold: pending from our Doctor. Status: "proposed for Mars".
2. Table for the project page: NASA hazard → NASA risk → what Sputnik does (radiation → dosimeter + NASA DONKI solar storm card; isolation → mood step, private diary, friendly chat; sleep → bracelet every night; distance → Mars mode, works offline; gravity → BP vs personal norm, vision check; closed environment → CO₂, SpO₂, temperature).
3. Bracelet source: NASA ARCHeR (Artemis II, wrist actigraphy for sleep, motion, light).
4. Precedent: NASA tested Tempus Pro telemedicine; our Earth screen extends it for Mars with delayed communication.
5. Earth benefit: polar stations, submarines, offshore rigs, remote villages; CSA: research helps with balance in older adults, osteoporosis, heart disease, type 2 diabetes.
