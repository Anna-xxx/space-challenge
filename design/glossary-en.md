# Sputnik UI glossary, English (1 Nov 2026)

Source: Anna's English version of design/slovar-i-pravki-tekstov.md, section 1, with the Writer's edits marked ✎.
English uses American spelling (NASA, the challenge brief and the jury use US English) ✎.

| Concept | Use | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Result color | **zone**: green, yellow, orange, red. "Blood pressure in the yellow zone", "Morning check: yellow zone" | color of the day, yellow day, yellow level | "Yellow level" reads like a threat level. "Zone" is familiar from traffic lights and heart-rate zones |
| Whole morning procedure | **morning check**, short **morning** | morning kit, check-up, check-in | "Kit" is the equipment bag |
| Equipment bag | **kit** ("kit by the crew quarters") | equipment bag, medical bag, pack | One term everywhere |
| One morning task | **step** ("Step 2 of 5") | stage, item | |
| What a device measures | **reading** (result on screen), **measurement** (the act) | result, check | Mood and the reaction test are steps, not readings |
| Personal reference value | **your normal** ("Your normal: 118/76") ✎ | baseline, standard value, normal range | One short form fits every metric; "range" is wrong for a single number like 37.3 |
| Value inside it | **normal** on tiles, **within your normal** in sentences ✎ | as usual, OK, within range | Matches the Russian «в норме» |
| Mood 1–5 | **usually 4**, "as usual" | normal | Mood has no medical normal |
| Module CO₂ | **usually 2.5** | normal | Station value, not personal |
| Final morning screen | **morning summary** | report, daily result, overview | "Report" is only what goes to the doctor: "Your doctor got the report" |
| Doctor on Earth | **flight surgeon** (Elena S.); "your doctor" in casual lines ✎ | medic, Mission Control | "Flight surgeon" is the real NASA title for an astronaut's doctor; the jury will recognize it |
| Medic on the station | **crew medical officer**, short **CMO** ✎ | flight medic, doctor | Real ISS role name; "flight medic" is not used at NASA |
| Assistant | **Sputnik**, as a name, no quotes: "Sputnik says", "Sputnik, emergency" | AI, assistant | Speaks as "I" |
| Symptom labels | **symptoms** ("Anything bothering you?" + symptom chips) ✎ | tags, markers | "Markers" means biomarkers to a doctor |
| Voice | **Voice recordings aren't saved** ✎ | your voice is not recorded | Audio is recorded for speech recognition, then deleted |
| Exit | **Log out** ✎ | close profile, log out of profile | Standard button; "of profile" is extra |
| Emergency button | **hold for 2 seconds** | keep pressed, press and hold | One instruction on all screens |
| Device status | **Waiting for the cuff → Connected, waiting for numbers → Got it** ✎ | | Same three states as Russian |

## Notes

- Jokes and step captions («манжета, твой звёздный час» and others) are not translated. Write them fresh in English: puns don't carry over (BA rule, ba/proverka-ekrana-utro-1-1.md).
- Steps start with a verb, as in Russian: "Put the cuff on your bare upper arm, 2 cm above the elbow."
- No exclamation marks, second person, short lines.
